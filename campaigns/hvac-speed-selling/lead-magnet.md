## PAGE 2 — QUICK WIN #1: THE 60-SECOND LEAD RESEARCH

Here's something you can do today that changes how fast you respond to every new lead.

When a call comes in, you need three things before you call back:
1. **Property size** (tells you system tonnage)
2. **Year built** (tells you likely system age)
3. **Home value** (tells you budget range)

Most contractors either skip this entirely or spend 15-20 minutes Googling, checking Zillow, pulling tax records.

**The faster way:** Use AI to pull all of this in one shot.

Give an AI tool the address. In under 60 seconds you get:
- Square footage and lot size
- Year built and likely original HVAC install date
- Home value (so you know their budget range)
- Local energy costs and utility rates
- Available rebates and tax credits in their area
- A speed-to-lead response text you can send immediately

**What this looks like in practice:**

> Lead comes in: "3847 Oakwood Dr, Memphis TN 38128"
>
> 60 seconds later you know: 1,850 sq ft, built 1998, likely original system (26+ years), home value $185K, Shelby County has MLGW rebates for heat pumps.
>
> You text: "Hey, this is [Name] from [Company]. Got your message — I work on a lot of homes in your area around that age. Most of them are running the original system. I have some time Thursday morning, does that work?"

That text hits different when you clearly know something about their house. They feel like you're already paying attention.

**Do this today:** Next lead that comes in, look up the address on Zillow before you call back. Note the year built and square footage. Reference it in your callback. See how the conversation changes.

## PAGE 4 — THE 3-TIER ESTIMATE FRAMEWORK

Here's where most HVAC contractors leave money on the table.

You show up, measure the house, check the existing system, and give them one number. One option. Take it or leave it.

The problem: when you give one option, the only decision is yes or no. And "no" is easier than "yes" when someone is looking at a $7,000-$12,000 bill they weren't expecting.

**The fix: always present three tiers.**

### Good — "Gets the Job Done"
- Standard efficiency equipment (14-15 SEER2)
- Basic warranty
- Reliable brand, nothing fancy
- **Position:** "This is a solid system. It'll cool your house and last 15+ years."

### Better — "The Smart Upgrade"
- Higher efficiency (16-17 SEER2)
- Extended warranty
- Better brand or feature set (variable speed blower, quieter operation)
- Include applicable rebates and financing
- **Position:** "This is what most of my customers go with. Better efficiency means lower bills, and the rebate knocks $600-$800 off the price."

### Best — "The Premium Setup"
- Top efficiency (18+ SEER2 or heat pump)
- Full warranty + maintenance plan
- Smart thermostat, zoning, or indoor air quality add-on
- All rebates + financing options
- **Position:** "This is the top of the line. Lowest energy bills, longest warranty, and it qualifies for every rebate available."

**Why this works:**
- 60-70% of homeowners pick the middle option
- The "Good" option anchors the price low so "Better" feels reasonable
- The "Best" option makes "Better" look like a deal
- Nobody feels pressured — they're choosing, not being sold

**Pro tip:** Always present the numbers with monthly financing, not just the lump sum. "$187/month" lands differently than "$8,500."

## PAGE 5 — THE FOLLOW-UP SYSTEM THAT ACTUALLY WORKS

You gave the estimate. They said "let me think about it." Now what?

Most contractors do one of two things:
1. Call once, get voicemail, never call again
2. Forget entirely because three more calls came in

Both of those lose you money.

**Here's the follow-up cadence that works:**

| When | What | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Same day (2-3 hrs after estimate) | Text: "Hey [Name], wanted to make sure you got the estimate. Any questions I can clear up?" | Strike while it's fresh |
| Day 2 | Text: Share a relevant tip — energy savings calc, rebate deadline, or maintenance tip | Add value, stay top of mind |
| Day 4 | Call: "Just checking in — have you had a chance to look things over?" | Voice builds trust |
| Day 7 | Text: Share a customer story — "Just finished an install on [Street Name], similar house to yours" | Social proof, urgency |
| Day 10 | Text: "Hey [Name] — I've got my schedule filling up for [month]. If you're still thinking about it, I can hold a spot. No pressure either way." | Real urgency (not fake) |
| Day 14 | Text: "Should I close out your estimate, or are you still considering? Either way is fine." | The breakup text — highest reply rate |

**Why this matters:**
- 80% of sales require 5+ follow-ups
- Most contractors stop after 1
- The contractor who follows up wins the job — even if their price is higher
