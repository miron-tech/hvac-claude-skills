## HOOK 4 — How-To Hook (LinkedIn / Facebook)

Here's how an HVAC contractor responds to a lead in under 60 seconds — without stopping what they're doing:

1. Lead comes in (call, form, whatever)
2. Automated text fires: "Hey, this is [Name] from [Company]. I'm on a job right now — what can I help with?"
3. Meanwhile, AI pulls the property data: sq ft, year built, home value, likely system age
4. When you're ready to call back, you already know their situation
5. You reference their house on the callback: "I work on a lot of homes in your area around that age..."

That's it. The homeowner feels like you're already paying attention. Your competitors are still Googling the address.
