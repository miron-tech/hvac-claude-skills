Quick story.

I ran service businesses for 20+ years. Mobile detailing. Managed five car wash locations. The whole time, the same thing kept happening — leads would come in and nobody followed up fast enough.

Not because we were lazy. Because we were on jobs. Doing the actual work.

"I know I'm losing jobs. I just can't get back to people fast enough."

So I built something that does the follow-up before you even see the notification. Researches the lead, sends the first text, and has the property data ready when you call back.

Not because the technology is cool. Because I've been the guy on the job site missing calls, and I know what that costs.
