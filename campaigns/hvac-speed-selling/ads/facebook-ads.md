## TARGETING SUGGESTIONS

**Cold audiences:**
- Job title: HVAC contractor, HVAC technician, HVAC business owner
- Interests: HVAC, heating and air conditioning, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Jobber
- Behaviors: Small business owners

**Warm audiences:**
- Website visitors (landing page)
- Video viewers (75%+)
- Page engagers (90 days)

**Lookalike:**
- 1% lookalike of lead magnet downloaders
- 1% lookalike of RankGrid customers

## BUDGET RECOMMENDATION

- **Test phase:** $20-30/day across 3 ad variations for 7 days
- **Scale:** Double budget on winner, kill losers after 1,000 impressions with no conversions
- **Target CPL:** $5-15 for lead magnet download (cold), $2-5 (retargeting)
