# Review Engine Skill

> **Purpose:** After every completed job, get a 5-star review. Then respond to it in a way that feeds your Google ranking. Repeat until you have 300+ reviews at 4.7+ stars and you're the #1 HVAC contractor in your market.

---

## WHAT THIS SKILL DOES

- Generates **personalized review request messages** (text + email) based on job type and customer interaction
- **Times the request** optimally based on job type
- Provides **follow-up sequence** if customer doesn't leave a review (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7)
- Writes **review response templates** for positive AND negative reviews, personalized to the actual review
- Embeds **SEO keywords** in review responses (service type + city name) to boost Google Business Profile
- Tracks **review velocity benchmarks** — where you stand vs. what you need to dominate local search
- Coaches on the **HEARD framework** for handling negative reviews
- Recommends **QR code** and **direct Google review link** strategies for in-person jobs

## WHAT THIS SKILL DOES NOT DO

- Does not auto-post reviews or responses (contractor reviews and sends)
- Does not fake or incentivize reviews (against Google TOS — will get you penalized)
- Does not handle BBB, Yelp, or platform-specific review strategies (Google focus)
- Does not remove negative reviews (only Google can do that, and only for policy violations)

## FRAMEWORK: The Review Flywheel

### Step 1 — Time the Ask

| Job Type | When to Ask | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| Emergency repair | Within 1-2 hours after completion | Emotional gratitude is highest right after you saved them. "You fixed my AC in July" = 5 stars. |
| Replacement / installation | Next morning (12-18 hours after) | Let them enjoy the new system overnight. Wake up to perfect temperature = positive emotion. |
| Maintenance / tune-up | Same day, 2-4 hours after | Quick job = quick ask. Don't wait — they'll forget by tomorrow. |
| Duct cleaning | Same evening | They notice the difference in air quality after a few hours. |

### Step 2 — Send the Request

**Always include a direct Google review link.** Don't make them search for you.

**Google review link format:**
```
https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=YOUR_GOOGLE_PLACE_ID
```
> To find your Place ID: Search your business on Google Maps → click your listing → look at the URL or use Google's Place ID Finder tool.

**Review Request Templates:**

#### After Emergency Repair
```
"Hey [Name] — glad we got your [AC/furnace] back up and running today! If you
have 30 seconds, a Google review would mean the world to us. Most of our
customers find us through reviews, and yours helps the next person in the
same spot find us too.

[Direct Google Review Link]

Thanks for trusting us with your home. 🙏"
```

#### After Replacement / Installation
```
"Good morning [Name]! How's the new [system type] treating you? Hope you
slept well in that perfect [temperature] last night.

If you're happy with how everything went, a quick Google review would really
help us out. Takes about 30 seconds:

[Direct Google Review Link]

Thanks again for choosing [Company Name]. Enjoy the new system!"
```

#### After Maintenance / Tune-Up
```
"Hey [Name] — thanks for letting us tune up your system today. Everything
looked [good/note specific finding]. You're all set for [summer/winter].

If you have a quick second, a Google review helps us a ton:

[Direct Google Review Link]

Appreciate your business!"
```

#### After Any Job (Generic)
```
"Hi [Name], thanks for choosing [Company Name] for your [service type] at
[address]. If you had a good experience, a quick Google review helps other
homeowners in [city] find us:

[Direct Google Review Link]

Thanks!"
```

### Step 3 — Follow Up If No Review

| Touch | Timing | Message |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Initial request | Per timing above | Full request with link |
| Follow-up 1 | Day 3 after job | "Hey [Name] — just a quick reminder about the review. No worries if not, but it really does help: [link]" |
| Follow-up 2 | Day 7 after job | "Last note from me on this — if you had a good experience, we'd really appreciate a review. If there's anything we could've done better, I'd rather hear it directly: [your phone/email]. [link]" |
| Stop | After Day 7 | Don't ask again. 3 touches is the max before it feels pushy. |

**Conversion expectations:**
- Text + direct link: 8-12% conversion rate
- Email only: 3-5% conversion rate
- In-person ask + QR code: 10-15% conversion rate (highest)
- Without follow-up: 2-4% conversion rate

### Step 4 — Respond to Every Review

**Respond to EVERY review — positive and negative.** This is where most contractors fail. 63% of reviews go unresponded to. Responding shows Google you're active AND builds trust with future customers reading reviews.

**Response timing:** Within 24-48 hours of the review being posted.

#### Positive Review Response Templates

**Key: Include your service type + city/area naturally.** This is free SEO content on your Google Business Profile.

**After AC Replacement:**
```
"Thanks so much, [Name]! It was great working with you on the AC replacement
at your [neighborhood/area] home. That new Carrier system is going to keep you
comfortable for years — and those energy savings are going to show up on your
first summer bill. We appreciate you trusting [Company Name] for your HVAC
needs in [City]. Enjoy the cool air!"
```

**After Furnace Repair:**
```
"Really appreciate the kind words, [Name]! We know how stressful it is when
the heat goes out in [City] — glad we could get your furnace back up and
running quickly. If you ever need anything, we're just a call away. Thanks
for choosing [Company Name] for your heating repair!"
```

**After Maintenance:**
```
"Thanks for the review, [Name]! Regular maintenance is the best way to keep
your HVAC system running efficiently — glad we could get you squared away for
[season] in [City/Area]. See you next [season] for the next tune-up!"
```

**General (customize):**
```
"[Name], thank you! We take a lot of pride in our [service type] work here
in [City], and reviews like yours remind us why we do what we do. Really
appreciate you taking the time. Don't hesitate to reach out if you ever
need anything!"
```

> **SEO hack:** Every response that naturally mentions "HVAC," "AC replacement," "furnace repair," "heating," "cooling," or your city name adds keyword-rich content to your Google Business Profile. Don't keyword-stuff — keep it natural. But always mention the service and the city.

#### Negative Review Response — The HEARD Framework

**H**ear — Acknowledge the specific complaint
**E**mpathize — Show understanding without being defensive
**A**pologize — Take responsibility where appropriate
**R**esolve — Offer to make it right (move offline)
**D**iagnose — Internal process to prevent recurrence (don't share publicly)

**Template:**
```
"[Name], I'm sorry to hear about your experience with [specific issue they
mentioned]. That's not the standard we hold ourselves to at [Company Name].

I understand how frustrating [specific situation] must have been, and I take
full responsibility for making it right. I'd love the chance to talk with you
directly and resolve this — please call me at [phone] or email [email] at
your convenience.

We're committed to getting this right for you."
```

**Rules for negative review responses:**
- Respond within 24 hours — speed shows you care
- Never argue or get defensive publicly
- Never reveal private details about the job
- Always offer to move the conversation offline
- Be specific about what went wrong (shows you actually read the review)
- Keep it professional — future customers are reading this
- If the review is fake or violates Google policy, flag it for removal through Google's process AND still respond professionally

## QR CODE STRATEGY

**For in-person jobs (repairs, installations, tune-ups):**

1. Create a QR code that links to your direct Google review URL
2. Print it on a leave-behind card that the tech hands to the homeowner
3. Card says: "How'd we do? Scan to leave a quick review" + QR code + company logo

**Why QR codes work:**
- No friction — scan and review in 30 seconds
- In-person ask + physical card = 10-15% conversion
- Tech hands it at the moment of highest satisfaction (job just completed, system working)
- Homeowner has their phone in their hand already

**Leave-behind card content:**
```
[Company Logo]

Thanks for choosing [Company Name]!

If we earned it, we'd love a quick Google review.
It takes 30 seconds and helps your neighbors find us too.

[QR CODE]

Or visit: [short URL]
```

## TECHNICIAN'S ROLE IN REVIEWS

The tech is the most trusted person. Their in-person ask is 2-3x more effective than a text.

**What the tech says before leaving:**
"Everything's running great now. If you were happy with the service today, it would really help us if you could leave a quick Google review. I'll leave this card with the QR code — just scan it with your phone camera. Takes about 30 seconds. We really appreciate it."

**Key rules for techs:**
- Only ask if the customer is clearly satisfied (don't ask angry customers for reviews)
- Ask BEFORE leaving, not after (once you leave, the moment is gone)
- Be genuine, not scripted (customers can tell the difference)
- Hand them the QR card as you ask
- If the customer seems neutral or unhappy, DON'T ask. Instead: "Is there anything else I can help with before I go?"

## DECISION LOGIC

```
IF job just completed + customer happy → Send review request (timed by job type)
IF job just completed + customer had concerns → Resolve concern FIRST, then ask for review
IF job just completed + customer upset → DO NOT ask for review. Resolve the issue. Use HEARD.
IF review posted + positive → Respond within 24-48 hrs with keyword-rich response
IF review posted + negative → Respond within 24 hrs using HEARD framework
IF review request sent + no review in 3 days → Send Follow-up 1
IF review request sent + no review in 7 days → Send Follow-up 2 (final)
IF customer left review → Do NOT send follow-up requests
IF tech is on-site and customer is happy → Tech asks in-person + leaves QR card
IF monthly velocity < 5 reviews → Something is broken. Check: are requests going out? Using direct link? Following up?
DEFAULT → Send personalized request timed by job type, with direct Google link
```
